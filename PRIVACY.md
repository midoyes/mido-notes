# 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy — Mido筆記 (Mido Notes)

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-28

## 中文

Mido筆記是個人用的離線加密記事本，沒有自己的伺服器。

- **資料存放**：筆記、帳號密碼與附件都以 AES-256-GCM 加密後存放在你自己的裝置上。開發者無法讀取，也不會收集、傳送或分享你的任何資料。
- **不含追蹤**：App 不含廣告、分析或追蹤程式。
- **Google 雲端硬碟同步（選用，預設關閉）**：只有在你自己於設定中登入 Google 並開啟同步時才會啟用。App 只要求 `drive.appdata` 權限，也就是只能存取 App 自己在你 Google 雲端硬碟中的專用隱藏資料夾，看不到你其他的檔案。上傳的是在你的裝置上先加密過的檔案，Google 與開發者都無法讀取其內容。登入憑證只保存在你的裝置上，你可以隨時在設定中登出，或到 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」移除授權，並刪除該隱藏資料夾中的同步檔。
- **其他網路使用**：除上述選用的同步外，App 不會主動連線。
- **聯絡**：midoyes@gmail.com

## English

Mido Notes is a personal offline encrypted notebook with no server of its own.

- **Data storage**: Notes, credentials and attachments are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored on your own device. The developer cannot read them and does not collect, transmit or share any of your data.
- **No tracking**: The app contains no ads, analytics or trackers.
- **Google Drive sync (optional, off by default)**: Enabled only if you sign in to Google and turn it on in Settings. The app requests only the `drive.appdata` scope, i.e. access to the app's own hidden data folder in your Google Drive; it cannot see your other files. What is uploaded is a file already encrypted on your device, which neither Google nor the developer can read. Sign-in credentials are kept only on your device; you can sign out in Settings at any time, or revoke access in your Google Account ("Third-party access") and delete the sync file in that hidden folder.
- **Other network use**: Apart from the optional sync above, the app does not connect on its own.
- **Contact**: midoyes@gmail.com
